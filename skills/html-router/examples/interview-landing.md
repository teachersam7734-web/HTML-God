# Interview: pottery workshops landing page
Request: I'm trying to make a landing page for my small pottery studio's new weekend workshops. I want people to sign up.
Mode: tool
Already answered: deliverable (a landing page), primary action (sign up), licence mode (commercial: a studio's own business), language (English, the request's language)

Message before round 1: "Before I build, eight quick questions in two rounds. If you skip one, I take the default I name.
For question 1: if you have photos, a logo or text about the workshops, I use them exactly instead of inventing; you
can drop files into the project folder, paste text, or give links. For question 4: one file works like a document you
can email or open with no internet, while a website lives at an address and is easy to share by link but needs
somewhere to host it."

## Round 1
### Q1 [Materials]
Explain: If you have photos of your pottery, a logo or text about the workshops, I use them exactly instead of inventing. You can drop files into the project folder, paste text here, or give me links.
Ask: Do you have photos, a logo or workshop details (dates, price, what people make) to put on the page?
Default: If you have no preference, I will write the text from your request, use drawn illustrations instead of photos, and mark every invented detail for you to check.
Options:
- I will share files or text (Recommended): you add photos, logo and details next
- Write it for me: I invent clearly marked sample details
- A mix: some material now, the rest written for you
Fills: brief.sources, brief.assets

### Q2 [Look]
Ask: Which feel suits the studio best?
Default: If you have no preference, I will design three different looks from the world of clay and kilns and show them side by side, so you pick before I build.
Options:
- Show me three looks first (Recommended): three versions of the top of the page, side by side
- Warm and hand-made: earthy colours, paper and clay textures, soft light
- Bright editorial: cream paper, elegant headings, like a fine magazine
- Bold and playful: strong colours, chunky shapes, confident type
Fills: brief.look

### Q3 [Brand]
Ask: Does the studio already have colours or fonts it always uses?
Default: If you have no preference, I will choose colours and fonts from the clay, glaze and kiln world of the subject.
Options:
- No, choose for me (Recommended): colours and fonts made for this page
- Yes, I will share them: I follow them exactly
- Only a logo: I build the colours around it
Fills: brief.brand

### Q4 [Delivery]
Explain: One file works like a document: you can email it or open it with no internet. A website lives at an address, is easy to share by link, but needs somewhere to host it.
Technical: yes
Ask: Should this be one file you can send and open anywhere, or a website at your own address?
Default: If you have no preference, I will make one file that works with no internet, and show you how to put it online later.
Options:
- One file that works anywhere (Recommended): easy to send, opens with no internet
- A website I will put online: shared by link
- Both: the file plus a version ready to put online
Fills: profile.offline, profile.deliverable

## Round 2
### Q5 [Sign-up]
Explain: A page with no website behind it cannot send sign-ups by itself. It can save each sign-up on the visitor's device and open their email to send it to you; a page that is online can send sign-ups to a service you choose.
Technical: yes
Ask: When someone signs up, how should their details reach you?
Default: If you have no preference, I will open the visitor's email with their details filled in, addressed to you, and show a clear confirmation.
Options:
- By email, filled in for them (Recommended): works with no service to set up
- Through a sign-up service: tell me which one you use
- Just collect interest for now: a confirmation, nothing sent
Fills: brief.form

### Q6 [Extras]
Explain: 3D objects and moving light are drawn live by the visitor's device, which makes them striking, and I always include a still version for devices that cannot draw them. Sound only plays after a visitor taps, always with a mute button.
Ask: Would you like a showpiece at the top: a slowly turning 3D pot, a moving glaze-light background, a short video, or gentle sound?
Default: If you have no preference, I will make a slowly turning 3D pot with real light on its glaze, no sound.
Options:
- A turning 3D pot (Recommended): the memorable moment of the page
- A moving glaze-light background: colour that drifts like wet glaze
- A short video: from your own footage
- Keep it simple: beautiful still images only
Fills: brief.media, brief.memorable

### Q7 [Proof]
Ask: Do you have kind words from past students or numbers to show, such as "120 people taught"?
Default: If you have no preference, I will leave the proof section out rather than invent quotes.
Options:
- Leave it out for now (Recommended): no invented quotes
- Yes, I will share them: real quotes and numbers
Fills: brief.proof

### Q8 [Devices]
Explain: Rich effects need a capable device. I design for the devices your visitors actually use, not for the computer I build on, and every effect has a calm version for devices that cannot run it.
Technical: yes
Ask: Will most visitors arrive on phones, on computers, or both?
Default: If you have no preference, I will design it for phones first and make it just as good on tablets and laptops.
Options:
- Both, phones first (Recommended): checked at phone, tablet and laptop sizes
- Mostly computers: the big-screen layout leads
- Mostly phones: built for one hand and a small screen
Fills: profile.widths, brief.devices
