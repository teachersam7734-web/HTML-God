Hero directions for Tidewater Reading Club

Brief: an offline reading course for adults who stalled on long books; one action (start the free first week); proof is real member numbers. Same copy in every direction.

Design read: a reading course for busy adults, with a calm but confident language, leaning toward warm paper or deep ink / one serif or one grotesque / lively motion. Dials: variance 7, motion 6, density 4.

A. Editorial ledger

Thesis: the course sells reading, so the hero reads like a well-set page: type is the only image and proof sits in a ruled ledger.

Optimises trust and calm for adults who distrust gimmicks; gives up visual drama and a memorable picture.

  • Structure from long-form magazine openers: asymmetric headline column, a side column of figures.
  • Style recipe: the editorial cell of styles.html (serif display, hairlines).

B. Instrument panel

Thesis: progress is the product, so the hero is a dashboard you already want to fill: a reading dial is the one bold element and the proof is a stats bar.

Optimises motivation and a clear picture of the outcome; gives up warmth, and risks looking like a fitness app.

  • Structure from product heroes with a split screen: copy left, the product's own instrument right, a metrics strip below.
  • Style recipe: the dark mode good cell of styles.html (lighter-tint elevation, desaturated accent).

C. Poster

Thesis: stalled readers need a push, not a brochure: one saturated field, a centred shout and a single member's words as the proof.

Optimises energy and memorability on a phone; gives up the hard numbers above the fold and some seriousness.

  • Structure from campaign posters: centred stack, oversized words, one quote under the action.
  • Style recipe: the neo-brutalism cell of styles.html, softened (pill shapes, no hard shadow).

Hierarchy

A leads with the sentence; B with the sentence and the dial at equal weight; C with the sentence alone. B is the only one where the eye may land on the picture first.

Proof

A and B show the three numbers above the fold; C trades them for one human quote and leaves the numbers to the next section.

Risk

A can feel quiet next to competitors; B can feel like a tracker app; C can feel loud for a course about patience.
